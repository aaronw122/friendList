# Friend List — R3 Review Verdict (Judge, Final Round)

Inputs: PLAN.md · r3-consolidated (prosecution) · r3-challenge (defense) · r2-verdict (prior adjudication).
Severity bar: "the app still delivers its core promise" (friend-sent songs auto-added, privately, set-and-forget) — not "doesn't crash." R1/R2-settled items are not re-litigated absent genuinely new reasoning.

## Contested platform facts (judge's findings)

1. **`SMAppService.agent.register()` + `RunAtLoad` does start a second live instance.** `register()` loads the job into launchd, and per `launchd.plist(5)` a `RunAtLoad` job is "launched once at the time the job is loaded" — immediately, not at next login. If registration lands `.enabled`, the launchd instance (B) starts while the LaunchServices onboarding instance (A) is still running; if it lands `.requiresApproval`, the launch fires the moment the user approves — which the plan's own M4 flow ("Enable Friend List in Login Items") actively drives them to do. This is documented, reproduced behavior (Apple Developer Forums thread on SMAppService.agent launching a second instance). The plan's `flock` (Component 2, line 113) is explicitly scoped to preventing double-*writes*; it names no winner, no loser-exits contract, and does nothing about two `MenuBarExtra` processes. Prosecution correct on the fact.
2. **Boolean `KeepAlive=true` respawns after a clean Quit; `{SuccessfulExit=false}` is crash-only.** `launchd.plist(5)` on the boolean form: the job is kept alive *unconditionally* — any exit, including a deliberate `exit(0)` from the menubar Quit, is followed by a relaunch (throttled to launchd's default ~10s `ThrottleInterval`; "storm" is hyperbole, but "Quit does not stay quit" is exact). The dictionary form `KeepAlive={SuccessfulExit=false}` restarts only on abnormal/non-zero exit, preserving `RunAtLoad` login-start + crash-restart while honoring a deliberate Quit. The circulating claim that boolean `true` already respects exit 0 is a community misconception contradicted by the man page. Prosecution correct on the fact.
3. **A Spotify Development-Mode app OWNER does not need to add their own account under Users and Access.** The "user not registered in the Developer Dashboard" 403 applies to **non-owner** users of a dev-mode app; the allowlist ("each Spotify user who installs your app must be added") is a tester mechanism. The owner authenticates directly with the owning (Premium) account. The plan's BYO-client_id model is strictly owner==user — precisely the case needing no self-add. This is the identical fact the R2 judge found (R2 fact 3) when dismissing R2-M2. Defense correct; prosecution's premise false. (The dev-mode tester-cap change, 25 → 5, is real policy churn but touches nothing in this plan's single-user model beyond a copy nit — impl-note territory, and the "verify at M2 on a fresh dev app" gate already covers residual policy risk.)

## Per-issue verdicts

C1: UPHELD (Critical) - The fact is verified (fact 1) and the defense concedes after three failed disproof attempts. This is deterministic, not probabilistic: for every user, at the Done screen, `register()` (or the driven approval that follows `.requiresApproval`) starts a second live instance while the onboarding instance is still up — and since the app is menubar-only and onboarding merely "closes to the menubar," instance A never exits; both persist until logout. The harm is not cosmetic (two menubar icons): a duplicate instance attacks the exact single-process invariants the R1/R2-fixed plan now load-bears on — Component 5's refresh-token serialization is "an in-process lock (trivial now that everything runs in one process)" and Component 8's single-writer is "by construction." Two processes racing Spotify's single-use rotating refresh token means one refresh invalidates the other's stored token — recurring auth death and "Reconnect Spotify" on day one, the precise opposite of set-and-forget — plus racy double-adds ahead of dedup-cache sync, with the flock-loser's behavior unspecified rather than a clean exit. Deterministic, designed-in, silent-until-broken, on 100% of the onboarding path: that is above Must-fix, and the cheap fix (post-`register()` handoff — onboarding instance terminates; bundle-id `NSRunningApplication` activate-existing-and-exit on every launch; lock-loser exits 0) does not lower severity, per R2's own standard. NEW — introduced by the R2-endorsed `SMAppService.agent` mechanism, properly judged on merits. Fold the fix into M4 with an acceptance line: "complete onboarding → exactly one FriendList process and one menubar icon; Finder-open the .app while running → existing instance activates, second process exits."

MF1: UPHELD (Must-fix) - The fact is verified (fact 2) and the defense concedes. The plan specifies bare `RunAtLoad + KeepAlive` in four places (Architecture, Components 1 and 2, Tech stack), i.e. boolean `true`, so a deliberate menubar Quit is followed by a ~10s respawn — the user cannot make the app stay quit short of System Settings surgery. For a privacy-first app whose pitch is trust, "I quit it and it kept reading my messages database" is a genuine promise-breaker, and it also silently defeats the plan's own pause/quit affordances (Component 1's menubar Quit item becomes a lie). The fix is exactly as filed and small: `KeepAlive={SuccessfulExit=false}` (login-start + crash-restart preserved, clean Quit honored), route Quit through `SMAppService.unregister()`/bootout if "quit means don't come back at login" is intended (the plan must say which), keep pause as an in-process flag, and add an M4 acceptance line ("Quit from the menubar → process stays dead; `kill -9` → respawns"). NEW — introduced by the R2 fix; R2 named the watchdog but never specified the plist key, so this is the first adjudication.

MF2: DISMISSED - Re-raise of R2-M2, which the R2 judge dismissed on the identical false premise, and R3 brings no genuinely new reasoning: the only delta is the tester-cap number (25 → 5), which is real but irrelevant to whether an owner must self-add (fact 3 — they need not). The plan's "no allowlist" line is correct for its owner==user model. The defense's DISPROVEN verdict is sound. What survives is what already exists: the M2 milestone's live-write verification on a fresh dev-mode app (which would catch any future Spotify policy change in this area) and an impl-note to update any copy stating the old 25-user cap. No onboarding self-add step is added.

MF3: DOWNGRADED (Low — unchanged from R1-M7/R2-M6) - Third filing of the same wording seam, tagged "carried-over" by the prosecution itself, with no new argument; twice already downgraded to Low. The retry queue's chat/sender fields live in Application Support on the user's Mac and never leave it — the privacy *pillar* ("no user data ever leaves the Mac") is intact; the residue is the one-line tension with §Privacy's "never … contact identifiers beyond what filtering needs locally." The Low-scoped fix stands as previously ruled, with a preference now worth stating since a re-POST needs only the URI: drop chat/sender from retry entries (store track URI + attempt count) — it is both the cheaper edit and the better privacy posture — or amend the §Privacy sentence. The defense's DISPROVEN-as-Must-fix is sound; the underlying one-line item remains real at Low.

## Defense spot-check

Both CONCEDEs (C1, MF1) are fact-checked true — no overreach; if anything the defense under-sold C1 by not naming the refresh-token-rotation race as the worst consequence. Both DISPROVENs (MF2, MF3) survive scrutiny: MF2's disproof rests on the same Spotify quota-mode fact the R2 judge independently found, and MF3's disproof correctly distinguishes the intact off-device pillar from the local wording seam while conceding the Low residue. Nothing real was waved away.

## Holistic Assessment

R3 splits exactly as the defense frames it: the two genuinely new findings are both real and both are children of R2's own remedy — R2 blessed the `SMAppService.agent` + KeepAlive *mechanism* for launch survivability but left the plist semantics and process-lifecycle contract unspecified, and the plan wired both wrong (`RunAtLoad` with no single-instance handoff; boolean `KeepAlive`). The two carried-over findings are settled re-raises and fall as before.

Co-occurrence check: C1 and MF1 compound each other unpleasantly — with boolean KeepAlive, even a fix where the flock-loser "just exits" turns into a respawn loop (launchd relaunches the loser every ~10s, it loses the lock, exits, relaunches), so the single-instance contract and the `SuccessfulExit=false` change must land *together*, plus Quit routed through unregister. All three are small, well-specified plist/lifecycle edits confined to Component 1/2 and M4; none disturbs the architecture (single executable, in-process scanner, BYO client_id, watermark/dedup design), which three rounds have now failed to dent.

With C1 + MF1 applied and the standing Low items (MF3 wording, R1/R2 leftovers) folded in, the app delivers its core promise: friend-sent songs auto-added, privately, set-and-forget, surviving quits, crashes, logins, and its own onboarding. **Verdict on the plan: SOUND — ship-ready once M4 absorbs the single-instance handoff + `KeepAlive={SuccessfulExit=false}` + Quit-through-unregister work item, with the acceptance lines above. This closes the launch-survivability seam R2 opened; no further review round is warranted — remaining risk is empirical (M1 attributedBody, M2 dev-mode write gate), which milestones, not reviews, will retire.**
